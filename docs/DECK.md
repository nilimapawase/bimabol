# BimaBol pitch deck text (export to PDF)

## Problem and solution

Under PMFBY, farmers must report localised crop loss within 72 hours. Late intimation is a common rejection reason. BimaBol (७२ तास) is a Marathi-first voice PWA: speak the loss, confirm a record, see a conservative 72-hour clock, get the words for helpline 14447, save the SMS ticket, and keep evidence on the phone. It does **not** file claims.

## Who and how many

Insured smallholders in Maharashtra, family members, and service-centre operators. Press reports put unseasonal rain and hail at about 2,04,835 hectares across 29 districts (14 March–2 April 2026) and 61,004 hectares across 27 districts in May–June 2026. Not all of that land was insured. There is no count of farmers who miss the 72-hour window. **No pilot users yet.** The figures below are targets, not results.

## Depends on / what if costly or stops

No backend, no paid API, no login. Hosting cost stays small. Web Speech needs a network because the browser vendor recognises speech. If the microphone fails, the same steps can be typed. 14447, the Crop Insurance App, the bank, and the agriculture office remain the official route. A paid speech API is not part of this build.

## Launch plan

Time the same steps with 5–10 farmers first. A later pilot target is about 30 insured farmers in one district, helped by family or a service centre: at least 24 save a 14447 ticket within 24 hours of speaking, and at least 27 confirmed records contain crop, village, loss, and time. No farmer has used it yet.
